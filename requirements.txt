# Amazon Product Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Amazon product sales data to identify
patterns in product pricing, discounts, ratings, and customer reviews.

## Objectives
- Analyze product pricing and discounts.
- Identify top-rated product categories.
- Explore customer ratings and reviews.
- Understand the relationship between discounts and ratings.
- Generate meaningful business insights.

## Technologies Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook
- Streamlit
- Plotly

## Key Findings
- Average discounted price: ₹235.58
- Average original price: ₹249.46
- Average product rating: approximately 4.1
- Discount and product rating correlation: 0.028
- Reviews and product rating correlation: 0.098
